# Equipment Research: Corn Belt farmer-owned co-op, 1970–2030

Compiled 2026-10-25 for data-file use. No game, mod or game-wiki sources were used. About 70 web searches were run. Direct page fetches failed with DNS errors, so most facts come from search-result summaries of the pages listed in the source list.

**Confidence key:** **H** = confirmed by two or more independent or primary sources. **M** = one credible source, or consistent secondary sources. **L** = weakly sourced, or the researcher's estimate (labelled "estimate").
**Prices:** nominal US dollars, with the year given. Use the ENR index in section 14 to convert between years.

## 1. Commercial corrugated steel grain bins and tanks

### Size timeline (largest commercial diameter in common use)
| Era | Typical commercial tank | Largest available | Notes | Conf. |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1970 | 30–48 ft dia, 20–60k bu | ~60 ft | Steel annexes of about 30,000 bu "became common in the 1970s" beside concrete houses | M (Canadian Encyclopedia); diameters L estimate |

| 2015 | 90–135 ft, 0.5–1.3M bu | ~132–135 ft | Sioux Steel up to 132 ft, about 1M bu (Farm Progress) | M |
| 2019 | 105–156 ft | 156 ft | GSI 156-ft tank for Red Wing Grain (MN), Aug 2019; Brock 156-ft free-standing bin, 1.3M bu, Brainard NE (built with hydraulic jack lift) | H |
| 2020–21 | 135 ft common for 1.3M bu tanks | 165 ft (Sukup, Golden Grain Energy, Mason City IA; ground broken 14 Sep 2020; record 2021) | GSI Evo 2X rated up to 156 ft, just under 2M bu | H |
| 2025 | 105–156 ft standard; 1.0–1.35M bu single tanks common at co-ops | 165 ft | e.g., NEW Cooperative (Cooper, IA) 1.35M-bu GSI tank; Sublette IL 1.3M-bu Chief CB-45; Varna IL 1.3M-bu Sukup 135-ft | M |

**Hopper vs flat bottom:** Commercial tanks are flat-bottom, with sweep and floor unloading. Hopper tanks (45° cone, usually ≤ 48 ft diameter, usually ≤ ~30–40k bu) are used for wet holding, load-out surge and feed (estimate, L). Commercial hopper tanks are part of current Behlen and Brock lines (M).
