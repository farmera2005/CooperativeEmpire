# Regional research: three new playable regions

Prepared for the Agricultural Cooperative Tycoon (clean room). No game, mod or game-wiki sources were used.

**Conventions**
- **Conf.** = confidence (High / Medium / Low).
- **[BK]** = from background knowledge. These values were **not** confirmed by a source in this session. Treat them as starting tuning values and as `confidence: 'low'` or `'medium'`.
- **[S#]** = a source found this session (list at the end). Most sources were read only through search-result extracts. Direct fetches were blocked by the network: ncei.noaa.gov (403), ndsu.edu and climate-charts.com (DNS), and the sec.gov, ers.usda.gov and wikipedia.org pages linked by results. An entry marked [S#] therefore means "reported in a search extract". It does not mean the primary table was read.
- **Baseline.** "Central IL" means the existing Eastern/Central Corn Belt region. Yield ratios use **Illinois state averages** as the denominator, because those are the numbers that were available. Central IL runs about 3–5% above the IL state average [BK], so for a central-IL baseline multiply each ratio by about 0.96.
- **Region definitions** used for the shares:
  - **RRV** = ND counties Pembina→Richland (Walsh, Grand Forks, Traill, Cass, Richland) plus MN Kittson→Wilkin (Polk, Norman, Clay, Marshall).
  - **WCB** = central and eastern Nebraska (Platte valley), central Kansas, and western Iowa.
  - **GL** = the Michigan Thumb and Saginaw Valley (Huron, Tuscola, Sanilac, Bay, Saginaw) plus NW Ohio (Wood, Henry, Hancock, Sandusky, Seneca).

## 5. Gaps and caveats
1. **1970/1990 county-level crop shares and farm sizes** are [BK] estimates. The Census of Agriculture historical volumes (agcensus.library.cornell.edu) and NASS Quick Stats county series were not reachable or were not extracted. These are the highest-value items to verify.
2. **1991–2020 monthly HDD normals.** NCEI (403) and climate-charts (DNS) were blocked. Only Grand Island has an official 1991–2020 annual total. The Fargo figures are an older normal. Saginaw is a third-party sum.
3. **Weather-event probabilities and fieldwork-day monthly averages** are [BK], supported only by single-week samples. NDSU's circular "Potential field work days during planting and harvesting" (library.ndsu.edu/ir/handle/10365/9123) has the proper climatology, but the host was blocked.
4. **Basis spreads** are [BK] apart from one Feb 2026 DTN data point (ND processor −80H vs Decatur +3H). USDA AMS Grain Transportation Report basis tables would firm these up.
5. **Livestock shares and head per farm** are all [BK] except Michigan dairy 2017 (2,158 farms, 442 k cows) and Nebraska cattle on feed in 2025.
6. **Conflicts found:**
   - United Sugars founding year (1973 per one extract; [BK] says 1990s).
   - 2011 ND prevented-planting total (4.5 M vs ~6 M ac).
   - Exact UP–C&NW dates (control Apr 6 or May 1 1995; merger Sept 30 or Oct 1 1995).
7. **Real-company rule.** Per CLAUDE.md, every real co-op, railroad and lender above may only do what `history.ts` records. Negative random events (e.g. a Farmland-style failure) must target fictional entities. The PBB, Farmland bankruptcy and ACSC lockout items are real history and should only be scripted events.
